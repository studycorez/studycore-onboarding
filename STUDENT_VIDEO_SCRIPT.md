# Student Welcome Video Script
## "You Made the Right Call"
**Tone:** Direct, energetic, peer-level. Not a lecture. Speak to the student like you'd talk to a motivated 16-year-old.
**Length target:** 3–4 minutes
**Recorded by:** Neil (or Tanuj)

---

[OPEN — casual setting, direct to camera]

"Hey — if you're watching this, it means you just signed up for StudyCore. Good. Let me tell you what that actually means.

The SAT is not a test of how smart you are. It's a skill test. And skills are trainable. That's the whole point of why you're here.

Here's what the next few weeks look like:

First — you're going to take a diagnostic. That's a practice test that shows us exactly where you are right now. Don't stress about it. It's not graded, it's not going on your record, it just tells us where to focus.

Then, you're going to be matched with your tutor. Someone who scored in the top 1% on this exact test. They know every trick, every pattern, every shortcut. And every session you have with them is specifically built around your gaps.

Here's what I want you to understand: the students who hit their target scores are not always the ones who start the highest. They're the ones who show up consistently. Every session. Every homework assignment. They do the work.

You already made the hard decision — you signed up. Now the only thing between you and your target score is the work. And we're going to be with you every step of the way.

Fill out your onboarding form below, take your diagnostic, and let's get started.

You've got this."

---

**B-roll suggestions:** score reports, student at desk, phone with Zoom session open
**End card:** StudyCore logo + "Your journey starts now"
